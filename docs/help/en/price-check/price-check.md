# Check what is free and what it costs (Price check)

The **Price check** tab answers "is it free, and what does it cost?" on one card. Set the dates, search for a product, and see how many are free and its price, with your seasons, extras, a coupon and the returning-customer discount.

It only checks: "Only a check: nothing is booked or held, and no coupon use is counted." Making orders comes with the New order screen in a later update.

**Who can do this**

- Owners, managers and staff: the free counts and the prices.
- Mechanics and drivers: the free counts only. They never see a price, a coupon or a discount.

## Steps

1. Open the **Price check** tab.
2. At the top of the card, set the **Pickup** and the **Return**. They start as tomorrow at 10:00 to the day after at 10:00, on the location's clock.
3. If your shop has more than one location, choose the **Location**.
4. Under **Booked from**, choose one (it starts on **The counter**):
   - **The counter**: a booking staff make in the app. Your minimum notice does not apply, and the walk-in minimum days do.
   - **Your booking page**: a customer booking online. Your minimum notice and the booked-ahead minimum days apply.
   - **Moto Mangos**: a booking from Moto Mangos. Only vehicles switched on for Moto Mangos can be booked there, and the longer of your minimum notice and the vehicle's lead time applies.
5. In the search box, start typing a product's name, its kind (such as "Motorbike" or "Rental gear") or its category (such as "Scooter"). Pick the product from the list.
6. Its answer opens under the box: "N of M free" (a service shows "A service: nothing to count"), and a reason in red if it can't be booked.
7. Owners, managers and staff also see its price for those dates, titled for example "Honda Click 125: price from The counter". Type a **Coupon code** and press **Apply**, or tick **Returning customer**. To price more than one, or add extras, open **Quantity and extras** (just **Quantity** when the product has no extras). See [Check a price](../products/check-a-price.md) for the breakdown.
8. Changed the dates, the location or **Booked from**? Press **Check** to see the same product again for the new ones.

The last few products you looked at stay under the search box as **Recent** buttons, on this device. Tap one to check it again.

A coupon is judged for the channel you chose. A coupon that is only for your booking page is refused under **The counter** and applied under **Your booking page**.

## An example

A scooter has a flat fee of 300 baht per day, your prices include tax, and high season (20 December to 15 January) adds 20%. You check a pickup on 22 December at 10:00 and a return on 24 December at 10:00, under **The counter**, with the code WELCOME10 (10% off, for staff, can be used on season discounts). The breakdown shows the **List price** 600, the season +120 (2 days inside), the coupon −72, and a **Total** of 648 baht, which includes the VAT. Your own numbers depend on your prices, seasons and tax setting.

## Why a product can't be booked

- **"Too short: at least N days on this channel."** The rental is shorter than the minimum days: the walk-in minimum for **The counter**, the booked-ahead minimum online. See [Set your booking rules](../booking-rules/set-booking-rules.md).
- **"Too soon: the earliest pickup on this channel is …"** Your minimum notice (online only; on Moto Mangos, the vehicle's lead time if it is longer). Pick a later pickup, or choose **The counter**.
- **"Closed at the pickup time."** / **"Closed at the return time."** The location is closed then (opening hours or a closure).
- **"This location has no opening hours, so it takes no online bookings."** Set opening hours on the location. See [Set opening hours](../locations/set-opening-hours.md).
- **"Not listed on Moto Mangos."** The vehicle is not switched on for Moto Mangos.
- **"Moto Mangos has used all the units you list there for these dates."** Moto Mangos bookings already take every Instant and Request unit you list for this vehicle. Raise the counts on its Moto Mangos tab if you want to offer more.
- **"None free for these dates."** Every one is booked or blocked.
- **"None free. Overbooking is on, so staff could still save it as a shortage."** Only under **The counter**. Taking such a booking comes with orders in a later update, and a vehicle must be found before pickup. Online bookings are never overbooked.

## Common problems

- **"Nothing matches “…”."** No product at this location has that name, kind or category. Check the spelling, or try a shorter word. A product with nothing at this location isn't listed.
- **"The return must be after the pickup."** Set a later return.
- **"Nothing to rent at this location yet."** The location has no vehicles, gear or services yet.
- **"Add a location first."**
- The price says **"Fill in the prices to check one."**: the product has no price yet.
- The price says **"This rental can't be priced. Check the dates and the quantity."** Check the dates and the **Quantity**.
- A coupon that isn't applied says why: see [Why a coupon wasn't applied](../coupons/coupon-not-applied.md).
