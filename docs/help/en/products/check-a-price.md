# Check a price

**Check a price** sits on every product's **Pricing** tab. It prices a sample rental with the prices on screen, saved or not, using the same pricing as bookings, so Check a price and Save always agree. Here a coupon is judged as for a booking at the counter; to try one for your booking page or Moto Mangos, use the [Price check](../price-check/price-check.md), which opens the same **Check a price** with the saved prices.

Who can use it: owners, managers and staff.

## Steps

1. Open the product's **Pricing** tab and scroll to **Check a price**.
2. Under **Sample rental by**, choose **Number of days** or **Dates**.
3. Set the **Pickup** and either **Days** or **Return**.
4. Set **How many**.
5. Optional, for a vehicle with extras: under **Extras taken with it**, type how many of each extra go with the rental (0 to 99). An extra with some included free says "N included with each".
6. Optional: type a **Coupon code** and press **Apply**. Press **Remove the code** to take it off.
7. Optional: tick **Returning customer** to see the returning-customer discount.

The **Price breakdown** shows the **List price** and how it was worked out (for example "7 days from the price list"), any **Season**, extras, discounts, VAT ("Includes VAT 7%" or "VAT 7%"), the **Total**, and the **Deposit** ("Taken beside the total; never taxed.").

## Common problems

- **"Fill in the prices to check one."** The product has no price yet.
- **"This rental can't be priced. Check the dates and the quantity."**
- **"The shop’s returning-customer discount is off (Settings › Pricing and tax)."** See [Set up the returning-customer discount](../pricing-settings/returning-customer-discount.md).
- A coupon that isn't applied says why: see [Why a coupon wasn't applied](../coupons/coupon-not-applied.md).
