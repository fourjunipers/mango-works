# Change the quantity of an item for sale and read its stock history

An item for sale (a sticker, shirt or hat) has a **Stock** tab. For each location it shows how many you have on hand, for example "12 on hand". Below that, **Stock history** lists every change to what is on hand, newest first, up to the latest 20.

Who can do this: owners and managers change the quantity. Anyone who can open the product can read the Stock tab and the history; only owners and managers see the name of who made each change.

## Steps: change the quantity

1. Open **Products**, open the item and choose the **Stock** tab.
2. Find the location. In **Quantity**, type what you have on hand now ("What you have on hand now. The change is kept in the stock history.").
3. Press **Change quantity**.

You type the new total, not the difference: the history works out the difference for you. An item can be counted from 0 up to 100,000 per location.

## Read the stock history

Each line shows the kind of change and the amount, for example "Received +50":

- **Received**: the first quantity recorded for the item at that location.
- **Adjusted**: a later change, up or down, that you typed.
- **Sold** and **Returned**: reserved for orders, which are not built yet, so you won't see them today.

Under each line are the location (only when the shop has more than one), who made the change (owners and managers only) and when. It says "No stock recorded yet." until the first quantity is saved.

Services have no stock: they have no **Stock** tab and are always available.

## Common problems

- **"Enter a whole number."** Type a whole number from 0 to 100,000, with no decimals.
- **"Enter a quantity from 0 to 1,000 (up to 100,000 for an item for sale)."** The number is out of range.
- **"You can't make that change."** Only an owner or manager can change a quantity.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
