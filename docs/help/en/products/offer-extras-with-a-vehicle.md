# Offer extras with a vehicle

A vehicle product's **Extras** tab lists the rental gear and services offered with it. Online bookings take up to the most per vehicle; staff can add more at the counter.

An extra is a rental gear or service product with a price: see [Add rental gear, a service or an item for sale](create-gear-or-service.md). To offer something with every vehicle at once, use [Offer extras with every vehicle](../pricing-settings/extras-for-every-vehicle.md).

Who can do this: owners and managers. Everyone else can see the tab ("Only an owner or a manager can change extras.").

## Steps

1. Open the vehicle product and go to its **Extras** tab.
2. Under **Offered with this vehicle**, choose a product in **Add rental gear or a service** and press **Add**.
3. For each extra set:
   - **Included free**: how many come free with each vehicle, for example 2 helmets.
   - **Most per vehicle**: the most an online booking can take with each vehicle. Leave it empty for **No limit**.
4. Use the arrows to change the order, or remove a line with its remove button.
5. Under **From the list for every vehicle**, tick **Not offered for this vehicle** next to any shop-wide extra this vehicle shouldn't offer.
6. Press **Save extras**.

An extra marked "Retired: not offered" or "No price yet: not offered" is not offered until it is restored or given a price.

Changes are listed on the product's **History** tab.

## Common problems

- **"Enter a whole number in range."** **Included free** and **Most per vehicle** take whole numbers.
- **"The most per vehicle can't be below the number included."** Raise **Most per vehicle** or lower **Included free**.
- **"Check the lines marked below."** Fix the lines with an error.
- **"Check the extras and try again."**
- **"The shop has no rental gear or services yet. Add them on the Products page."**
- **"One of these extras no longer exists, or isn't rental gear or a service. Reload the page."**
- **"This vehicle no longer exists. Reload the page."**
- **"Someone is saving a booking for this vehicle right now, so nothing was saved. Please try again."** Wait a moment and save again.
- **"Only an owner or a manager can change extras."**
