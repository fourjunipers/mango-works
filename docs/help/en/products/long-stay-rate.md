# Set a long-stay daily rate (rentals over a month)

For a rental longer than 30 days:

- A month runs date to date from the pickup date (for example 5 March to 5 April).
- Each whole month costs the product's **monthly** price (the 30-day total).
- Each leftover day costs the **long-stay daily rate**. If you leave it blank, it is the monthly price ÷ 30.
- Leftover days never cost more than one more month.
- From 31 started days, a rental that is still short of its first calendar month (for example 1 January 10:00 to 31 January 12:00) costs the monthly price.

**Check a price** shows the working, for example "1 month and 15 days at {rate} a day", "2 months and 28 days: the extra days held to one more month at {monthly}" when the leftover days would cost more than a month, or "31 days started: the monthly price".

Who can do this: owners and managers. Staff can see the rate but not change it.

## Example

A Honda Click 125 with a **Monthly (30 days)** price of ฿6,000, picked up 5 March at 10:00 and returned 20 April at 10:00. That is 1 whole month (to 5 April) and 15 leftover days.

- Rate left blank: ฿6,000 ÷ 30 = ฿200 a day. Total ฿6,000 + 15 × ฿200 = ฿9,000.
- Rate set to ฿150: total ฿6,000 + 15 × ฿150 = ฿8,250.
- Returned 3 May at 10:00 instead (1 month and 28 days) with the rate at ฿250: 28 × ฿250 = ฿7,000 is more than a month, so the leftover days cost ฿6,000. Total ฿12,000.

Seasons can then raise or lower the price, as for any rental.

## Steps

1. Open the product's **Pricing** tab. It must be priced **By rental length** (its own prices or a shared price list).
2. Type the **Long-stay daily rate**. Leave it blank to use the monthly price ÷ 30.
3. Press **Save prices**.
4. Try a long rental in [Check a price](check-a-price.md): choose **Dates** and set a **Pickup** and **Return** more than a month apart.

## Common problems

- **"Enter an amount such as 200 or 1,234.50. Use a dot for decimals; a comma only between thousands (1,000). No spaces, and no more decimal places than the currency has."** Type the rate with a dot for decimals (12.50, not 12,50) and no spaces.
- **"A price can't be 0. To charge nothing, choose Free."** The rate can't be 0. Leave it blank to use the monthly price ÷ 30.
- **"You can't make that change."** Only owners and managers can change prices.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.

For how to type numbers, see [Typing amounts, percentages and other numbers](../general/typing-numbers.md).
