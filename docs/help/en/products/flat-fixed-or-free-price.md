# Set a flat fee, fixed price or free

Besides **By rental length**, a product can be priced:

| Method | What it means | Who can use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flat fee per period** | One amount per hour, day, week or month. Every started period is charged. A week is 7 days; a month runs date to date from the pickup. | Vehicles, rental gear, services |
| **Fixed price** | One amount per order, however long. | Rental gear, services, items for sale (their only method) |
| **Free** | Shown as included on the order. Nothing is charged. | Services only |

The Pricing tab only offers the methods the product can use. For example, a helmet (rental gear) offers **By rental length**, **Flat fee per period** and **Fixed price**.

Examples:

- A helmet at ฿20 **Per day**, rented for 3 days: ฿60.
- A phone holder at ฿100 **Per week**, rented for 10 days: 2 started weeks, ฿200.
- Delivery at a **Fixed price** of ฿200: ฿200 for any rental length.

Who can do this: owners and managers. Staff can see the prices but not change them.

## Steps

1. Open the product's **Pricing** tab.
2. Under **How it is priced**, choose the method.
3. For **Flat fee per period**, type the **Amount** and choose the **Period**: **Per hour**, **Per day**, **Per week** or **Per month**.
   For **Fixed price**, type the **Price**.
4. Rental gear only: optionally type a **Replacement value**, what a lost or broken item costs the customer. Leave it blank if you don't want one.
5. Press **Save prices**.

## Common problems

- **"Required."** The amount or price is missing.
- **"A price can't be 0. To charge nothing, choose Free."** An amount can't be 0. Only services can be **Free**; for rental gear, leave a **Replacement value** blank rather than 0.
- **"This kind of product can't be priced that way."** See the table above.
- **"Enter an amount such as 200 or 1,234.50. Use a dot for decimals; a comma only between thousands (1,000). No spaces, and no more decimal places than the currency has."** Use a dot for decimals and no spaces.
- **"You can't make that change."** Only owners and managers can change prices.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.

For how to type numbers, see [Typing amounts, percentages and other numbers](../general/typing-numbers.md).
