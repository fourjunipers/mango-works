# Add rental gear, a service or an item for sale

General products are everything that isn't a vehicle:

- **Rental gear**: lent out and returned, such as helmets, cameras and phone holders.
- **Item for sale**: sold, not returned, such as stickers, shirts and hats.
- **Service or extra**: charged for, with no stock, such as cover, delivery and licence help.

Gear and services can then be offered with your vehicles: see [Offer extras with a vehicle](offer-extras-with-a-vehicle.md).

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps

1. Open **Products** and press **New product**.
2. Under "What are you adding?", choose **General**.
3. Under "What type of product is it?", choose **Rental gear**, **Item for sale** or **Service or extra**.
4. Type the **Product name**. One product per size for now: add Helmet S, Helmet M and Helmet L as three products.
5. Optional: **Description**.
6. For gear and items for sale, type the **Quantity** you have at each location. You can change it later. For rental gear, tick **Track each unit on its own** if you want each one on its own line with a label (for example "GoPro 3"); leave it off to count them only.
   A service has no quantity: it is always available.
7. Press **Add product**.
8. Set its price on the **Pricing** tab: see [Set a flat fee, fixed price or free](flat-fixed-or-free-price.md).

## Common problems

- **"That name is already used in this shop."** Choose another name.
- **"Enter a whole number."** The quantity must be a whole number.
- **"Enter a quantity from 0 to 1,000 (up to 100,000 for an item for sale)."**
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
