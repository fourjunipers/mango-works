# Change or disconnect a bike's Moto Mangos model

Once a product is on a Moto Mangos list entry, its **Moto Mangos** tab shows "On the Moto Mangos list as" the model's name, with two buttons: **Change model** and **Disconnect**.

Neither one touches bookings already made. If the product's vehicles have upcoming bookings, a note above the buttons says so, for example "2 upcoming bookings on this product’s vehicles stay exactly as booked if you change the model or disconnect."

Who can do this: owners and managers. Other roles don't see the **Moto Mangos** tab.

While a request you sent is still waiting for review, neither button is shown.

## Change the model

Use this when the product is listed as the wrong make or model.

1. Open the product's **Moto Mangos** tab and open **Change model**.
2. Under **Select the new make and model**, choose the **Make** and the **Model**. The model the product is on now is not in the list.
3. Press **Connect**.

Your listing switch and its quantities stay as they are.

## Disconnect

Use this to take the product off Moto Mangos.

1. Open the product's **Moto Mangos** tab.
2. Press **Disconnect**. You are asked: "Disconnect this product from Moto Mangos? Its listing is switched off and its list entry is cleared. Bookings already made stay as they are." Confirm it.
3. To list it again later, connect a model again: see [Connect a motorbike to Moto Mangos](connect-a-bike-to-moto-mangos.md).

## Common problems

- **"It is already on that one. Choose a different one."** You picked the model the product is on already.
- **"Choose a catalog entry from the list."** Pick both a **Make** and a **Model**.
- **"This product isn’t on the Moto Mangos list."** It was disconnected already. Reload the page.
- **"Its request is still waiting for review."** A request that Moto Mangos hasn't reviewed yet can't be disconnected. Wait for the decision.
- **"Only an owner or manager can change that."** or **"You can't make that change."** Ask an owner or manager.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
