# Choose the tax on a product

A product's **Pricing** tab has a **Tax** card only when the shop has at least one tax profile (removed profiles don't count). With none, there is no Tax card and the shop charges no tax: add one first, see [Add, change or remove a tax profile](../pricing-settings/tax-profiles.md).

**Tax on this product** offers:

- **Shop default**, showing the shop's default profile, such as "Shop default (VAT 7%)", or "Shop default (no tax)" when the shop has no default.
- The shop's other tax profiles.
- **Not taxable**.

Whether prices include tax or have it added on top is a shop setting: see [Choose whether prices include tax](../pricing-settings/tax-inclusive-or-added.md). [Check a price](check-a-price.md) shows the tax on a sample rental, for example "Includes VAT 7%".

Who can do this: owners and managers. Staff can see the tax choice but not change it.

## Steps

1. Open the product's **Pricing** tab.
2. Under **Tax**, choose from **Tax on this product**.
3. Press **Save prices**.

## Common problems

- **"That tax profile is no longer in use."** The profile was removed. Choose another one.
- **"You can't make that change."** Only owners and managers can change prices and tax.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
