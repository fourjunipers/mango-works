# See and change how much rental gear you have

The **Details** tab of a rental gear product shows its stock at each location (the location's name heads each group when the shop has more than one). Everyone who can open the product sees the tiles. Owners and managers can also change the quantity.

The tiles:

- **In stock**: units in use plus units in repair. Lost units are not counted.
- **Available now**: units free to rent right now.
- **In repair**: units out for cleaning or repair.
- **Lost**: only shown while at least one unit is lost.

Who can do this: owners and managers change the quantity. Everyone else reads the tiles.

## Steps: change the quantity

1. Open **Products**, open the gear product. The **Details** tab opens first.
2. Under **Change quantity**, type the new total in **Quantity**, or press the **One more** (+) and **One fewer** (−) buttons.
3. Press **Save quantity**. The button only appears once the number differs from what is saved.

Raising the number adds units. Lowering it removes only free units, never ones in repair, lost or booked. Gear can be counted from 0 up to 1,000 per location.

The **Stock** tab lists one row per unit with a status of **In use**, **In repair** or **Lost**. Gear that is counted gets names like "Unit 1". To give each unit its own name, see [Label each unit of rental gear](label-gear-units.md).

## Common problems

- **"Enter a whole number."** The quantity must be a whole number from 0 to 1,000 (no decimals). A number above 1,000 gives this message too.
- **"{count} units are in repair or booked, so the quantity can’t go below {count}."** That many units can't be removed because they are in repair or booked. Put them back in use, or wait until the bookings end. The message shows the real number in place of {count}.
- **"Enter a quantity from 0 to 1,000 (up to 100,000 for an item for sale)."** The number is out of range.
- **"You can't make that change."** Only an owner or manager can change the quantity.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
