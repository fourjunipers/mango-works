# See and change how much rental gear you have

The **Details** tab of a rental gear product shows its stock at each location (the location's name heads each group when the shop has more than one). Everyone in the team can open the product and see the tiles, under **Stock**. Owners and managers can also change the quantity.

The tiles:

- **In stock**: units in use plus units in repair. Lost units are not counted.
- **Available now**: units free to rent right now.
- **In repair**: units out for cleaning or repair.
- **Lost**: only shown while at least one unit is lost.

For example, a shop has 21 helmets: 1 is lost and 2 are in repair. The tiles show **In stock** 20, **Available now** 18, **In repair** 2 and **Lost** 1. **Available now** goes down while helmets are out on a booking.

Who can do this: owners and managers change the quantity. Staff, mechanics and drivers read the tiles; they don't see **Change quantity**.

## Steps: change the quantity

1. Open **Products**, open the gear product. The **Details** tab opens first.
2. Under **Change quantity**, type the new total in **Quantity**, or press the + (**One more**) and − (**One fewer**) buttons.
3. Press **Save quantity**. The button only appears once the number differs from what is saved.

Raising the number adds units. Lowering it removes only free units, never ones in repair, lost or booked. Gear can be counted from 0 up to 1,000 per location.

The **Stock** tab lists one row per unit with a status of **In use**, **In repair** or **Lost**. Gear that is counted gets names like "Unit 1". To give each unit its own name, see [Label each unit of rental gear](label-gear-units.md).

## Common problems

- **"Enter a whole number."** The box is empty, or has words, a decimal point or a minus sign. Type a whole number such as 12.
- **"Enter a quantity from 0 to 1,000 (up to 100,000 for an item for sale)."** The number is above 1,000. Rental gear can be counted up to 1,000 per location.
- **"N units are in repair or booked, so the quantity can’t go below N."** That many units can't be removed because they are in repair or booked. Put them back in use, or wait until the bookings end. The message shows the real number in place of N ("1 unit is ..." when it is one).
- **"You can't make that change."** or **"Only an owner or manager can change that."** Only an owner or manager can change the quantity.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
