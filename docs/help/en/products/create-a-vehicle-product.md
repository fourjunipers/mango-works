# Create a vehicle product (what you rent out)

A product is something you rent out, like a Honda Click 160. Each vehicle you own sits under its product. A product is known by its name: there are no separate make and model boxes. Put the make, model and years in the name if you want them.

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps

1. Open **Products** and press **New product** (or **New product** on the Fleet page).
2. Under "What are you adding?", choose **Vehicle**.
3. Under "What kind of product is it?", choose **Motorbike**, **Car**, **Off-road**, **Boat or jet ski** or **Other**. The form changes to fit.
4. Type the **Product name**, for example "Honda CB500X 2022-2024". No two products can have the same name.
5. Tick its **Categories**. You can choose several. They are required for a motorbike or a car, optional otherwise:
   - Motorbike: Scooter, Touring, Adventure, Sport, Classic, Off-road
   - Car: Sedan, SUV, Pickup, Van, Hatchback, Convertible
   - Off-road: ATV, UTV, Dirt bike
   - Boat or jet ski: Jet ski, Speedboat, Pontoon
6. Optional: **Description**, **Can be booked online**, **Buffer before pickup (hours)** and **Buffer after return (hours)** (decimals are fine: 0.5 is half an hour), **Minimum age** and **Licence class**.
7. Press **Save product**.

   If you came here from **Add vehicle** (with **Product not here? Add it first**), there is no "What are you adding?" choice, and the button reads **Save and add a vehicle** instead: it saves the product and takes you back to adding the vehicle.

The product opens with its tabs: **Details**, **Units** (its vehicles), **Pricing**, **Extras**, **Moto Mangos** (motorbikes only) and **History**. Next:

- [Add a vehicle to your fleet](../fleet/add-a-vehicle.md)
- [Set prices by rental length](price-by-rental-length.md)

To change a product later, open it, change the **Details** tab and press **Save**.

## Common problems

- **"That name is already used in this shop."** Choose another name, for example add the years.
- **"Required."** The name, or a category for a motorbike or car, is missing.
- **"Choose one of the categories in the list."** Pick a category shown for the chosen kind.
- **"Enter the hours as a number, like 2 or 0.5 (168 at most). Use a dot for decimals; a comma only separates thousands, so 0,5 is not read as a half."** Check the buffers: write half an hour as 0.5, not 0,5.
- **"Enter a whole number in range."** Check the minimum age: a whole number, with no decimal point.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.

For how to type numbers, see [Typing amounts, percentages and other numbers](../general/typing-numbers.md).
