# Connect a motorbike to Moto Mangos

Moto Mangos is the online marketplace where renters find bikes. Before a bike product can be listed there, it has to be tied to a make and model on the Moto Mangos list. The **Moto Mangos** tab on a motorbike product is where you do that. When it is done, switch the listing on: see [Switch a bike on or off Moto Mangos and set how many are offered](moto-mangos-listing.md).

The tab only appears on motorbike products. Cars, boats and other vehicle kinds don't have it, and nor do rental gear, items for sale and services. Moto Mangos lists motorbikes only, for now.

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps: your bike is on the list

1. Open **Products**, open the motorbike's product, then the **Moto Mangos** tab.
2. Under **Select your make and model from the list**, choose the **Make**. The **Model** list then shows that make's models, with the years each covers.
3. Choose the **Model** and press **Connect**. Nothing is reviewed: the list's details are used straight away.
4. The tab now says "On the Moto Mangos list as" and the model's name. The Listing part below it is ready to use.

## Steps: your bike isn't on the list

1. First choose a category for the product on the **Details** tab and save. Until then the tab only says to choose a category first.
2. On the **Moto Mangos** tab, open **Don’t see your bike above? Send us the details and we’ll get it added**.
3. Fill in **Make**, **Model**, **Engine size (cc)**, **Transmission** and **Year of your bike**. All five are needed. The year is one year, like 2023. The make and model start filled in from the product's name where possible.
4. Optional: add a **Note for the reviewer**.
5. Press **Send for review**.
6. The tab says the request is waiting for review. You can keep using the product meanwhile. When Moto Mangos approves it, the tab says when the model was added, and the product is listed automatically. (After the next platform update you switch the listing on yourself, as for any connected bike.)

## If Moto Mangos turns the request down

The tab shows the date it was reviewed and the reviewer's note (or says no note was left). Under "What to do next" you can:

- press **Edit and send again**, fix the details and press **Send for review again**; or
- press **Choose a model from the list instead**.

## Common problems

- **"Choose a category for this model and save it first, then send it."** Set a category on the **Details** tab, save, then come back.
- **"Choose a catalog entry from the list."** Pick both a **Make** and a **Model** before pressing **Connect**.
- **"This model is already on the Moto Mangos list, or waiting for review."** The product is already connected, or has a request open. Reload the page to see its state.
- **"Only a model on the Moto Mangos list can be listed there."** The product isn't on an approved model yet. Wait for the review, or choose a model from the list.
- **"Moto Mangos lists motorbikes only, for now."** Only motorbike products can be connected.
- **"You already have a product with this make and model."** Another product in your shop has the same make and model. Use that one, or change one of them.
- **"Enter an engine size from 1 to 20000 cc."** Correct the engine size.
- **"Enter a year, such as 2024."** The year must be between 1950 and 2100.
- **"Give the make, model, engine size, transmission and year first."** One of the five details is missing.
- **"This product is retired. Restore it first."** A retired product can't be connected: see [Delete or restore a product](delete-or-restore-a-product.md).
- **"Only an owner or manager can change that."** or **"You can't make that change."** Ask an owner or manager.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.

If the tab says "Moto Mangos didn't add this model, so it can't be listed. It is still tied to that request because you have another model of your own with the same make and model, and a shop can't have two.", choose a model from the list, or delete the duplicate product.
