# Set a deposit on a vehicle

The shop's deposit setting decides how deposits work: see [Choose how the shop takes deposits](../pricing-settings/deposit.md). A vehicle's own deposit box only appears when that setting is **Per vehicle**. Otherwise the **Deposit** card on the Pricing tab says what the shop takes, for example "The shop takes 20% of each order as a deposit." or "The shop takes no deposit.", with **Change in Settings** for owners and managers.

Deposits are taken beside the total. They are never taxed, discounted or changed by a season.

Who can do this: owners and managers. Staff can see the deposit but not change it.

## Steps

1. Open the vehicle product's **Pricing** tab.
2. Under **Deposit**, type the amount. It is taken per vehicle on an order.
   - Leave it blank to use the shop's amount per vehicle. When the shop has one, the help text shows it: "Blank uses the shop's amount: {amount}. 0: no deposit for this one."
   - Type 0 for no deposit on this product.
3. Press **Save prices**.

For example, a Honda Click 125 with a ฿3,000 deposit: an order for 2 of them takes a ฿6,000 deposit.

## Common problems

- **No Deposit card?** Only vehicles have a deposit. Rental gear, services and items for sale have no Deposit card on their Pricing tab.
- **"Enter an amount such as 200 or 1,234.50. Use a dot for decimals; a comma only between thousands (1,000). No spaces, and no more decimal places than the currency has."** Use a dot for decimals and no spaces.
- **"You can't make that change."** Only owners and managers can change prices.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.

For how to type numbers, see [Typing amounts, percentages and other numbers](../general/typing-numbers.md).
