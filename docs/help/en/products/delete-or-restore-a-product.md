# Delete or restore a product

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Delete a product

A vehicle product can be deleted only when it has no vehicles at all, sold and retired ones included, because they hold its history. Rental gear and items for sale must have no stock left, lost units included.

1. Open the product. You are on its **Details** tab.
2. At the very bottom, press **Delete product**.
3. Confirm "Delete this product? This can't be undone." If the product was waiting for a Moto Mangos review, the question reads "Delete this product? This can't be undone. Its Moto Mangos review request will be withdrawn."

You are taken back to the Products list.

## Restore a retired product

A retired product is hidden from the Products and Fleet lists and from "Add vehicle", and is not listed on Moto Mangos.

1. Open **Products**, choose **Show**: **Retired**, and open the product.
2. On its **Details** tab, under **Retired**, press **Restore product**.

## Common problems

- **"This product still has vehicles. Remove all its vehicles first."** Shown in a pop-up when you press **Delete product**. Delete the vehicles added by mistake. Vehicles you really had can't be deleted once booked; keep the product instead.
- **"This product still has stock. Set its quantity to 0, and mark any lost units found or remove them from stock, first."** Shown in a pop-up for rental gear and items for sale. Bring the stock to zero first: rental gear on its **Details** tab (**Change quantity**), an item for sale on its **Stock** tab.
- **"This product is retired. Restore it first."** Shown when you try to list a retired product on Moto Mangos or add a vehicle to it. Press **Restore product** first. Editing its details still works while it is retired.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
