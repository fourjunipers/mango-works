# Delete or restore a product

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Delete a product

A product can be deleted only when it has no vehicles at all, sold and retired ones included, because they hold its history. A general product must have no stock left.

1. Open the product.
2. At the very bottom, press **Delete product**.
3. Confirm "Delete this product? This can't be undone." If it was waiting for a Moto Mangos review, that request is withdrawn too.

## Restore a retired product

A retired product is hidden from the Products and Fleet lists and from "Add vehicle", and is not listed on Moto Mangos.

1. Open **Products**, choose **Show**: **Retired**, and open the product.
2. Under **Retired**, press **Restore product**.

## Common problems

- **"This product still has vehicles. Remove all its vehicles first."** Delete the vehicles added by mistake. Vehicles you really had can't be deleted once booked; keep the product instead.
- **"This product still has stock. Set its quantity to 0, and mark any lost units found or remove them from stock, first."** For gear and items for sale, bring the stock to zero on the **Details** or **Stock** tab first.
- **"This product is retired. Restore it first."** Press **Restore product** before changing it.
