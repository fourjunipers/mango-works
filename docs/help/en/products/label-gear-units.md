# Label each unit of rental gear

The **Stock** tab of a rental gear product has one row per unit, with its name and a status of **In use**, **In repair** or **Lost**. Gear that is only counted shows units as "Unit 1", "Unit 2" and so on. Gear that is tracked unit by unit can carry a label of your choice.

Who can do this: owners and managers. Everyone else in the team can read the **Stock** tab, but doesn't see the labels' boxes.

## Steps

1. If the **Stock** tab says "These units are counted, not named.", switch on **Track each unit on its own** first: press **Open Product details**, tick **Track each unit on its own** under **Product details**, and press **Save**. See [Add rental gear, a service or an item for sale](create-gear-or-service.md).
2. Open the product's **Stock** tab.
3. In the unit's row, type a **Label (optional)**, for example "GoPro 3" (up to 60 characters).
4. Press **Save label**. A unit with no label shows as "Unit" and its number. To remove a label, empty the box and press **Save label**.

Labels also name the unit in **Report a problem**, **Out of service** and the History.

If you switch **Track each unit on its own** off again, every label of the product is removed.

## Common problems

- **"Another unit of this product already has that label."** Choose another label.
- **"Too long."** A label can be 60 characters at most.
- **"Switch on “Track each unit on its own” first."** Someone switched tracking off while you had the page open. Reload the page.
- **"You can't make that change."** Only an owner or manager can label units.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
