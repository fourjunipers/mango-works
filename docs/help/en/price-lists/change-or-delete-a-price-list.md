# Change or delete a price list

The **Price lists** page shows each list with its 1-day price and how many products use it.

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Change a list

1. Open **Shop settings** › **Price lists** and choose the list.
2. **Products on this list** shows who follows it: "Saving changes the prices of these N products:".
3. Change the name or the prices.
4. Press **Save price list**. If other products use it, confirm "Saving changes the prices of N products. Save?"

Each product's **History** tab records the change, for example "Price list 300–3000 changed (also used by 4 other products)".

## Delete a list

1. Open the list.
2. Press **Delete price list** and confirm "Delete this price list? This can't be undone."

A list can't be deleted while a product uses it. Move those products to their own prices or another list first.

## Common problems

- **"This price list is used by a product, so it can't be deleted. Move those products to other prices first."**
- **"A list can't be deleted while a product uses it. Move those products to other prices first."**
- **"A list's kind can't be changed."** Create a new list of the other kind instead.
- See [Create a price list](create-a-price-list.md) for the price errors.
