# Change or delete a price list

The **Price lists** page shows each list with its 1-day price and how many products use it.

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Change a list

1. Open **Shop settings** › **Price lists** and choose the list.
2. **Products on this list** shows who follows it, for example "Saving changes the prices of these 4 products:" and their names. "No product uses this list yet." means nobody does.
3. Change the name or the prices.
4. Press **Save price list**. If products use it, confirm "Saving changes the prices of N products. Save?" **Saved** appears.

Changing only the name changes no price.

Each product's **History** tab records the change, for example "Price list 300–3000 changed (also used by 4 other products)".

## Delete a list

1. Open the list.
2. Press **Delete price list** and confirm "Delete this price list? This can't be undone."

The list says "Price list deleted."

While a product uses the list, the page says under the delete button: "A list can't be deleted while a product uses it. Move those products to other prices first." Move those products to their own prices or another list first.

The list's kind is shown with the note "A list's kind can't be changed." To switch between amounts and multipliers, create a new list.

## Common problems

- **"This price list is used by a product, so it can't be deleted. Move those products to other prices first."**
- **"A price list's kind can't be changed."** Create a new list of the other kind instead.
- See [Create a price list](create-a-price-list.md) for the price errors.
