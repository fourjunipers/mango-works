# What each role can do

Everyone in a shop's team has one role: **Owner**, **Manager**, **Staff**, **Mechanic** or **Driver**. The role decides which tabs and pages you see, and what you can change. You can see your own role on **You and this device** ("Your role here: …"). To change someone's role, see [Change a team member's role](change-a-role.md).

This page lists what each role can do **today**. Orders, rentals, customers, check-in and check-out, payments and inviting people come in later updates.

## Who can do this

Everyone can read this page. Only owners and managers change roles.

## Tabs and pages

| | Owner | Manager | Staff | Mechanic | Driver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Orders** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| **Price check** | With prices | With prices | With prices | What is free, no prices | What is free, no prices |
| **Maintenance** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes (opens here) | No |
| **Products** and **Fleet** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes (read only) |
| **Locations** | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Team** | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Shop settings** | Yes | Yes | **Extras** only, read only | No | No |

A mechanic opens the app on **Maintenance**; everyone else opens on **Dashboard**. The **Orders** tab is there, but the orders themselves arrive in a later update.

Staff have **Shop settings** in the menu (the sidebar, or **You and this device** on a phone): it opens **Settings › Extras**, which they can read but not change. The link on a vehicle's **Extras** tab leads there too.

| See vehicles and their bookings | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Print QR stickers | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## Products and prices

| | Owner | Manager | Staff | Mechanic | Driver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Create, change, delete or reorder products | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| See prices, the **Pricing** tab and **Check a price** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Change prices, price lists, seasons, tax and deposits | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| See which extras go with a vehicle (**Extras** tab) | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Change the extras offered with a vehicle | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Moto Mangos listing and catalog requests | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| A product's **History** | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Coupons (**Settings › Coupons**) | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Booking rules (**Settings › Booking rules**) | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |

On a vehicle product, owners and managers see every tab. Staff see **Units**, **Pricing** and **Extras**. Mechanics and drivers see **Units** and **Extras**.

## Shop admin

| | Owner | Manager | Staff | Mechanic | Driver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Add, change or delete locations, hours and closures | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Change a location's time zone | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Shop details and tax and insurance reminders | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Change roles and remove people | Anyone | Staff, mechanics and drivers only | No | No | No |
| Make someone an owner or a manager | Yes | No | No | No | No |

A shop always keeps at least one owner.

## Common problems

- **A tab or page is missing.** Your role doesn't include it. Ask an owner or manager if you need it.
- **"Page not found"** ("It may have moved, or you don't have access to it.") You opened a page your role can't see, for example from a link someone sent you.
- **"You can't make that change."** Your role doesn't allow that change, or your role changed while the page was open. Reload the page.
