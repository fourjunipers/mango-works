# Change a team member's role

**Team** lists everyone who works in the shop, with their name, email and role.

## Who can do this

Owners and managers.

- Owners can give any role to anyone, including making another owner.
- Managers can only change staff, mechanics and drivers, and can only give those three roles. For an owner or another manager (and for yourself, as a manager) the page shows the role as plain text, with no box to change it.

## Steps

1. Open **Team**. It is under **Shop admin**: in the sidebar on a computer, or on the **You and this device** page on a phone (see [Finding your way around](../account/switch-shop-and-device.md)).
2. Find the person. You are marked "(you)".
3. In the role box next to their name, choose the new role.
4. Press **Save**. When it worked, you see **Saved**.

To see what each role can see and do, read [What each role can do](what-each-role-can-do.md).

## Common problems

- **"A shop needs at least one owner. Make someone else an owner first."** You tried to change the last owner to another role. Make another person an owner, then try again.
- **"You can't make that change."** Your role doesn't allow it, or the person's role changed meanwhile. Reload the page and try again.
- **"That didn't work. Please try again."** Something went wrong while saving. Reload the page and try again.
