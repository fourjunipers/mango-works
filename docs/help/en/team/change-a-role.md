# Change a team member's role

**Team** lists everyone who works in the shop, with their name, email and role.

Who can do this: owners and managers. Owners can give any role. Managers can only change staff, mechanics and drivers, and can only give those three roles.

## Steps

1. Open **Team**. It is under **Shop admin**: in the sidebar on a computer, or on the **You and this device** page on a phone (see [Finding your way around](../account/switch-shop-and-device.md)).
2. Find the person. You are marked "(you)".
3. In the role box next to their name, choose the new role.
4. Press **Save**.

## What each role can do

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including changing a location's time zone and making other owners |
| Manager | Everything in shop admin except the owner-only items; can change staff, mechanics and drivers |
| Staff | The four staff-app tabs. Updates odometers, vehicle status (Active or Maintenance), downtime and tax and insurance records. Sees products, fleet and prices but can't change product setup or prices |
| Mechanic | Dashboard, Price check (what is free, no prices) and Maintenance; updates odometers, vehicle status, downtime and tax and insurance records. Can't see prices |
| Driver | Dashboard, Orders and Price check (what is free, no prices). Can see the fleet but can't change vehicles |

## Common problems

- **"A shop needs at least one owner. Make someone else an owner first."** You tried to change or remove the last owner. Make another person an owner, then try again.
- **"You can't make that change."** Your role doesn't allow it, or the person's role changed meanwhile. Reload the page and try again.
- **"That didn't work. Please try again."** Something went wrong while saving. Reload the page and try again.
