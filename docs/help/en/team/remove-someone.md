# Remove someone from the team

## Who can do this

Owners and managers. Owners can remove anyone. Managers can remove staff, mechanics and drivers; next to an owner or a manager they see no **Remove** button.

## Steps

1. Open **Team**. It is under **Shop admin**: in the sidebar on a computer, or on the **You and this device** page on a phone.
2. Find the person and press **Remove**, next to their role.
3. Confirm "Remove {name} from the team?".

They can no longer open this shop. If they belong to other shops, those are not affected. To bring them back later, ask Mango Works to add them again (inviting people yourself comes in a later update).

## Common problems

- **"A shop needs at least one owner. Make someone else an owner first."** You can't remove the last owner. Make someone else an owner first.
- **"You can't make that change."** Your role doesn't allow removing this person.
- **"That didn't work. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
