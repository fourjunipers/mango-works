# Remove someone from the team

Who can do this: owners can remove anyone. Managers can remove staff, mechanics and drivers.

## Steps

1. Open **Team**.
2. Find the person and press **Remove**.
3. Confirm "Remove {name} from the team?".

They can no longer open this shop. If they belong to other shops, those are not affected.

## Common problems

- **"A shop needs at least one owner. Make someone else an owner first."** You can't remove the last owner. Make someone else an owner first.
- **"You can't make that change."** Your role doesn't allow removing this person.
- **"That didn't work. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
