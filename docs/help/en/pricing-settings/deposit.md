# Choose how the shop takes deposits

The deposit is what an order asks as security. It is never taxed or discounted.

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps

1. Open **Shop settings** and choose **Pricing and tax**.
2. Under **Deposit**, choose one:
   - **No deposit**: orders take no deposit. Deposits saved on vehicles are kept for later. A new shop starts here.
   - **A percentage of the order**: of the order's total after discounts, tax included. Type the **Percentage of the order**, for example 20 or 12.5.
   - **A fixed amount per order**: the same amount however many vehicles. Type the **Amount per order**. It is taken once per order.
   - **Per vehicle**: each vehicle's own deposit, set on its Pricing tab, times how many. Optionally type a **Shop amount per vehicle** for vehicles with no deposit of their own (blank: none).
3. Press **Save pricing settings**. **Saved** appears.

For example, with **A percentage of the order** at 20%, an order that comes to 1,070 baht (after discounts, tax included) asks a deposit of 214 baht. With **Per vehicle**, two scooters with a deposit of 1,000 baht each ask 2,000 baht.

With **Per vehicle**, set each vehicle's deposit: [Set a deposit on a vehicle](../products/vehicle-deposit.md).

**Check a price** on a product, and the **Price check**, show the deposit beside the total.

Taking the deposit from a customer comes with orders and payments in a later update.

## Common problems

- **"Required."** Type the percentage or amount for the option you chose.
- **"Enter a percentage such as 10 or 12.5. Use a dot for decimals, not a comma."**
- **"Enter a percentage above 0."**
- **"At most 1,000%."**
- **"Enter an amount such as 200 or 1,234.50. Use a dot for decimals; a comma only between thousands (1,000). No spaces, and no more decimal places than the currency has."**
- **"A price can't be 0. To charge nothing, choose Free."** A fixed deposit per order can't be 0: choose **No deposit** instead.
- **"Enter the deposit's percentage or amount."**
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.

For how to type numbers, see [Typing amounts, percentages and other numbers](../general/typing-numbers.md).
