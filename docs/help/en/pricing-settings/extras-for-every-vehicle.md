# Offer extras with every vehicle

**Settings › Extras** holds "The rental gear and services offered with every vehicle of the shop." A vehicle can set its own, or switch one off with "Not offered for this vehicle", on its Extras tab: see [Offer extras with a vehicle](../products/offer-extras-with-a-vehicle.md).

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps

1. Open **Shop settings** and choose **Extras**.
2. Under **Offered with every vehicle**, choose a product in **Add rental gear or a service** and press **Add**.
3. Set **Included free** and **Most per vehicle** (empty: **No limit**).
4. Use the arrows to change the order.
5. Press **Save extras**.

Changes are listed under **History** on the same page.

## Common problems

- **"Enter a whole number in range."** **Included free** and **Most per vehicle** take whole numbers.
- **"The most per vehicle can't be below the number included."**
- **"Check the lines marked below."** / **"Check the extras and try again."**
- **"The shop has no rental gear or services yet. Add them on the Products page."**
- **"One of these extras no longer exists, or isn't rental gear or a service. Reload the page."**
- **"Only an owner or a manager can change extras."**
