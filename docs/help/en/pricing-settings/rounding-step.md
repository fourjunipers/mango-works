# Set the rounding step

When quick price entry fills in the days between your prices, it rounds them to the shop's rounding step. Prices already saved never change.

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps

1. Open **Shop settings** and choose **Pricing and tax**.
2. Under **Rounding**, type the **Rounding step**, for example 10. Leave it blank to use the default for your currency (the help text shows it).
3. Press **Save pricing settings**.

Changing it only affects days filled in from now on.

The first time the shop saves **Pricing and tax**, you are asked: "Saving fixes the shop's currency as {currency}, and it can't be changed after that. Save?"

## Common problems

- **"Enter an amount, such as 45000 or 45000.50."** Type the step as a number.
- **"The currency can't change once prices have been saved in it."**
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
