# Set up the returning-customer discount

The returning-customer discount is taken off rentals automatically for a customer who has rented here before. It is taken off vehicles and rental gear priced by rental length or by a flat fee per period. It isn't taken off items for sale, services, or anything with a fixed price, and it isn't stopped by the **Accept coupons** switch.

For example, at 10% off, a scooter rental of 900 baht for 3 days comes to 810 baht. A rain poncho you sell as an item for sale keeps its full price.

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps

1. Open **Shop settings** and choose **Pricing and tax**.
2. Under **Returning-customer discount**, tick **Give returning customers a discount**.
3. Type the **Percentage off**, such as 10 or 12.5.
4. Type the **Completed rentals needed**: how many rentals at this shop make a customer "returning". Usually 1.
5. Press **Save pricing settings**. **Saved** appears.

Untick the box to switch it off. The percentage is kept for next time.

Customers and their past rentals come with orders in a later update. Until then, you try the discount by hand with the **Returning customer** tick (below).

## With seasons and coupons

The discount is taken after seasonal prices. Each coupon then decides whether it can be combined with this discount ("Can be combined with the returning-customer discount"). If not, the customer gets whichever of the two takes more off. See [Create a coupon](../coupons/create-a-coupon.md).

## Try it

Tick **Returning customer** in **Check a price** on any product's Pricing tab, or on the **Price check** tab. See [Check a price](../products/check-a-price.md). While the discount is off, the tick says "The shop’s returning-customer discount is off (Settings › Pricing and tax)."

## Common problems

- **"Enter the percentage to switch the discount on."**
- **"Enter a percentage such as 10 or 12.5. Use a dot for decimals, not a comma."**
- **"Enter a percentage above 0."**
- **"At most 100%."**
- **"Enter a whole number from 1 to 1,000."** Check **Completed rentals needed**.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.

For how to type numbers, see [Typing amounts, percentages and other numbers](../general/typing-numbers.md).
