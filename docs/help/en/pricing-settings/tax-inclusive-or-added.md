# Choose whether prices include tax

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps

1. Open **Shop settings** and choose **Pricing and tax**.
2. Under **Tax**, choose **Prices and tax**:
   - **Our prices include tax**: the price a customer sees is what they pay; the tax is worked out inside it. A new shop starts here.
   - **Tax is added on top**: the prices you type are before tax; the tax is added to the total.
3. Choose the **Default tax profile**, used by every product set to "Shop default". Choose **No tax** to charge none.
4. Press **Save pricing settings**. **Saved** appears.

If the shop has no tax profiles yet, the page says "Add a tax profile below to charge tax. Until then the shop charges no tax." See [Add a tax profile](tax-profiles.md).

## An example with VAT 7%

- **Our prices include tax**: a rental priced 1,070 baht costs the customer 1,070 baht, of which 70 baht is VAT.
- **Tax is added on top**: a rental priced 1,000 baht costs 1,000 baht plus 70 baht VAT, so 1,070 baht.

Tax is worked out on what is left after seasons and discounts, once per invoice and rate, not line by line. Tax is rounded once, to the smallest coin (halves round up). **Check a price** on a product shows it as "Includes VAT 7%" or "VAT 7%".

## Common problems

- **"That tax profile is no longer in use."** Choose another default.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
