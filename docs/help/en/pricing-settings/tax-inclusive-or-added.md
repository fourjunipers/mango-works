# Choose whether prices include tax

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps

1. Open **Shop settings** and choose **Pricing and tax**.
2. Under **Tax**, choose **Prices and tax**:
   - **Our prices include tax**: the price a customer sees is what they pay; the tax is worked out inside it.
   - **Tax is added on top**: the prices you type are before tax; the tax is added to the total.
3. Choose the **Default tax profile**, used by every product set to "Shop default". Choose **No tax** to charge none.
4. Press **Save pricing settings**.

If the shop has no tax profiles yet, the page says "Add a tax profile below to charge tax. Until then the shop charges no tax." See [Add a tax profile](tax-profiles.md).

Tax is worked out once per invoice and rate, not line by line. **Check a price** on a product shows it as "Includes VAT 7%" or "VAT 7%".

## Common problems

- **"That tax profile is no longer in use."** Choose another default.
- **"The currency can't change once prices have been saved in it."**
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
