# Scan a vehicle's QR sticker

Each vehicle's QR sticker opens that vehicle's page, where you can update the odometer, change the status, add downtime or record tax and insurance. It only works for people signed in to your shop: anyone else gets "Page not found".

Who can do this: everyone in the shop. The **Scan QR** button is on the **Maintenance** tab, which owners, managers, staff and mechanics have. Drivers don't have that tab: they scan with the phone's camera app instead.

To print stickers, see [Print QR stickers for your vehicles](print-qr-stickers.md).

## Scan in the app

1. Open the **Maintenance** tab.
2. Press **Scan QR** (the camera button beside **Search**). On a narrow phone screen it shows only the camera icon.
3. The first time, your browser asks to use the camera: allow it.
4. "Point the camera at the sticker. The vehicle opens as soon as the code is read." Hold the phone steady over the sticker.
5. The vehicle's page opens.

To stop, press **Close** (or Esc on a keyboard). The camera picture stays on your device: nothing is uploaded.

## Scan with the phone's camera app

1. Open your phone's own camera app and point it at the sticker.
2. Tap the link that appears.
3. If you aren't signed in on that browser, sign in first: the vehicle's page opens after you sign in.

## Common problems

- **"That code isn't one of your vehicle stickers. Keep the camera on a sticker."** The camera read another QR code. Point it at the vehicle's own sticker.
- **"The camera is blocked for this site. Allow it in the browser's site settings, then try again."** You said no to the camera earlier. Allow the camera for this site in your browser's settings, then press **Scan QR** again.
- **"This browser can't use the camera here. Use your phone's camera app instead: it opens the same sticker."** Scan with the camera app (see above).
- **"The camera couldn't be opened. Close other apps using it and try again."** Another app, such as a video call, is using the camera.
- **"Page not found"** ("It may have moved, or you don't have access to it."): the sticker belongs to a vehicle of a shop you aren't a member of, or the vehicle was deleted. Check you are signed in with your own account for this shop.
- The sticker won't read: it may be dirty, scratched or faded. Find the vehicle by typing its plate in **Find a vehicle** on the Maintenance tab instead, and print a new sticker.
