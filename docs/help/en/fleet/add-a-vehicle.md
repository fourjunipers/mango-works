# Add a vehicle to your fleet

A **product** is what you rent out, such as "Honda Click 160". A **vehicle** is one real bike or car of that product, with its own plate. Add the product first ([Create a vehicle product](../products/create-a-vehicle-product.md)), then add each vehicle to it.

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps

1. On **Fleet**, open the product's line and press **Add vehicle** under its vehicles, or press **Add vehicle** on the product's **Units** tab. Either way the product is already chosen.
2. Choose the **Product**. Only your own products are listed. If it isn't there, press **Product not here? Add it first**: you come back here after saving it.
3. Fill in what you know:
   - **Year**: the year of this vehicle.
   - **Odometer now** (in your shop's km or miles).
   - **Plate**. A new vehicle not registered yet: tick **Plate pending (not registered yet)** instead. Staff are warned before check-out.
   - **Colour**, **Frame (chassis) number**, **Engine number**.
   - **Location**: where the vehicle is kept.
   - **Notes**: anything else worth knowing.
4. Press **Save**. The vehicle's page opens.

To add many vehicles at once, use [Import vehicles from a CSV file](import-vehicles-csv.md).

## Common problems

- **"Enter the plate, or tick “Plate pending”."** Every vehicle needs a plate or the Plate pending tick.
- **"Another vehicle in this shop already has that plate."** Plates are unique in the shop. Check you aren't adding the same vehicle twice; search for the plate on **Fleet**.
- **"Add a product first. Then come back to add the vehicles you have of it."** The shop has no vehicle products yet. Create one first.
- **"Enter a year, such as 2024."** Type the four-digit year.
- **"Enter a distance, such as 12345."** Type the odometer as a number, without units.
- **"This product is retired. Restore it first."** Open the product and press **Restore product**.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
