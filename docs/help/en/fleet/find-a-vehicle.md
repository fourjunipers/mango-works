# Find a vehicle in your fleet

**Fleet** shows one line per product, with how many vehicles it has and how many are available now ("3 vehicles · 2 available"). A line opens to list its vehicles: plate, odometer, status, year and, with more than one location, the location. Tap a vehicle to open its page.

Who can do this: everyone in the shop (owners, managers, staff, mechanics and drivers) can see and search the Fleet. Only owners and managers can reorder it. The **Maintenance** tab search is for everyone except drivers.

## Search

1. Open **Fleet**.
2. In the search box (**Product name, plate or frame number**), type a product name, plate, frame number or engine number, and press **Search**.

Searching a product's name shows all its vehicles. Searching a plate, such as "1234", shows only the matching vehicles. The lines open by themselves while you search or filter.

## Filter

- The chips above the list filter by kind (**Motorbike**, **Car**, **Off-road**, **Boat or jet ski**, **Other**) and by category. **All** clears them. Only the kinds and categories your shop has are shown.
- Press **Filters** to choose **Show** (**Current fleet**, **Sold and retired** or **Everything**), **Product**, **Status**, **Location** (when you have more than one) and **Year**. Press **Apply**. The button shows how many filters are on, for example "Filters (2)". **Reset view** clears them.
- The "N sold or retired" link under the title shows the vehicles no longer in the fleet.

## Sort and reorder

The buttons above the list sort it: **A–Z**, **By type** or **My order**.

Owners and managers can set **My order**:

1. Press **Reorder**. The list switches to **My order**, without search or filters.
2. Drag a line, or use the arrows to move it one row at a time.
3. Press **Done**.

The same order is used on the Products list. To clear it, press **Reorder**, then **Reset to A–Z**, and confirm "Reset to A–Z? Your own order of products is cleared, on Fleet and on the Products list."

The search, filters and sort are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark a view.

## On a phone

On the **Maintenance** tab, type a plate, frame or engine number in **Find a vehicle** and press **Search**, or press **Scan QR** to scan the vehicle's sticker: see [Scan a vehicle's QR sticker](scan-a-qr-sticker.md). With nothing typed, Maintenance lists the vehicles in **Maintenance** status. **See the whole fleet** opens **Fleet**.

## Common problems

- **"No vehicle matches “{query}”."** Check the spelling, or search part of the plate.
- **"No vehicles match these filters."** Press **Reset view**.
- **"No vehicles yet. Add a product on the Products page, then add your vehicles to it, or import them all from a CSV file."** The shop has no vehicles yet: see [Add a vehicle to your fleet](add-a-vehicle.md).
- There is no **Reorder** button: your role isn't owner or manager, or the shop has only one product.
- A sold or retired vehicle is missing: set **Show** to **Sold and retired** or **Everything**.
