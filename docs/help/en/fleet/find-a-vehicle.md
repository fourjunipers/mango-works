# Find a vehicle in your fleet

**Fleet** shows one line per product, with how many vehicles it has and how many are available now ("3 vehicles · 2 available"). A line opens to list its vehicles: plate, odometer, status, year and, with more than one location, the location.

Everyone in the shop can see the Fleet.

## Search

1. Open **Fleet**.
2. In **Find a vehicle**, type a product name, plate, frame number or engine number, and press **Search**.

Searching a product's name shows all its vehicles. Searching a plate shows only the matching vehicle.

## Filter

- The chips above the list filter by kind (Motorbike, Car, Off-road, Boat or jet ski, Other) and by category.
- Press **Filters** to choose **Show** (**Current fleet**, **Sold and retired** or **Everything**), **Product**, **Status**, **Location** (when you have more than one) and **Year**. Press **Apply**. **Reset view** clears them.
- The "N sold or retired" link under the title shows the vehicles no longer in the fleet.

## Sort and reorder

**Sort** offers **A–Z**, **By type** and **My order**. Owners and managers can set **My order**: press **Reorder**, then drag a line, or use the arrows to move it one row at a time, and press **Done**. The same order is used on the Products list. **Reset to A–Z** clears it.

The search, filters and sort are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark a view.

## On a phone

On the **Maintenance** tab, type a plate, frame or engine number in **Find a vehicle** (the box says "Plate, frame or engine number"), or press **Scan QR** to scan the vehicle's sticker.

## Common problems

- **"No vehicle matches “{query}”."** Check the spelling, or search part of the plate.
- **"No vehicles match these filters."** Press **Reset view**.
- **"No vehicles yet. Add a product on the Products page, then add your vehicles to it, or import them all from a CSV file."** The shop has no vehicles yet: see [Add a vehicle to your fleet](add-a-vehicle.md).
