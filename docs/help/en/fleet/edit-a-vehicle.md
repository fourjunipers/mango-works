# Change a vehicle's details or move it to another location

Each vehicle has its own page: open **Fleet**, find the vehicle and tap it.

Who can do this: owners and managers. Staff and mechanics see the details but change only the odometer, status, downtime and tax and insurance records. Drivers see "You can view this vehicle but not change it."

## Steps

1. Open the vehicle's page.
2. Under **Details**, change what you need: **Product**, **Year**, **Odometer (km)** (or **Odometer (mi)**), **Plate**, **Plate pending (not registered yet)**, **Colour**, **Frame (chassis) number**, **Engine number**, **Location** or **Notes**.
3. Press **Save**. "Saved" shows next to the button.

## Moving a vehicle that has bookings

If the vehicle has upcoming bookings and you change its **Product** or **Location**, you are asked to confirm, for example "This vehicle has an upcoming booking. It will move with it to Chiang Mai Old Town." or "This vehicle has 3 upcoming bookings. They will move with it to Honda Click 125." The bookings move with the vehicle in the same save: either everything is saved or nothing is.

## A lower odometer reading

If you type a lower odometer reading, you are asked "Are you sure? This is lower than the current reading ({current} {unit})." Confirm only if the new reading is right. The change is kept in the product's **History** (see [Read a product's History](../products/product-history.md)).

## Common problems

- **"Another vehicle in this shop already has that plate."** Plates are unique in the shop.
- **"Enter the plate, or tick “Plate pending”."** Fill in one or the other.
- **"This vehicle was changed by someone else while this form was open. Reload the page to see the latest, then make your change again."** Someone saved this vehicle after you opened it. Reload and make your change again.
- **"This vehicle's bookings changed while this form was open. Reload the page to see them, then make your change again."** A booking was added or moved meanwhile. Reload and try again.
- **"This product is retired. Restore it first."** You chose a retired product. Choose another, or restore that product first.
- **"This location is no longer here. Reload the page."** The location was deleted meanwhile. Reload and choose another.
- **"This unit is no longer here. Reload the page."** The vehicle was deleted meanwhile.
- **"Enter a year, such as 2024."** Type the four-digit year, with no decimal point or comma.
- **"Enter a distance, such as 12345."** Type the odometer as a plain number: 12345 or 12,345, with a dot for a decimal. A comma anywhere else, such as 12,5, is refused.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Nothing was saved. This also shows when a booking for this vehicle was being saved at the same moment. Wait a moment, reload the page and try again.
