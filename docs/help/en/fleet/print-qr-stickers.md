# Print QR stickers for your vehicles

Every vehicle has its own QR sticker: "Scan it with a phone camera to open this vehicle. It only works for people signed in to your shop." Each printed sticker shows the QR code, the plate (or "Plate pending") and the product and year, for example "Honda Click 125 · 2024".

Who can do this: everyone in the shop (owners, managers, staff, mechanics and drivers).

## Print one sticker

1. Open the vehicle's page and find **QR sticker**.
2. Press **Print this sticker**. The **Print stickers** sheet opens with this vehicle already ticked.
3. Press **Print**, and print from your browser's print window.

## Print a sheet of stickers

1. Open **Fleet** and press **Print stickers**. (The button appears once the shop has vehicles.)
2. To narrow the list, choose a **Product** and a **Sort by** order (**Most recently added** or **Plate**), then press **Apply**. **Reset view** shows the whole list again.
3. Tick the vehicles to print, or tick **Select all N listed** ("Select the 1 vehicle listed" when there is only one). "N selected" shows how many are ticked. Only the ticked stickers appear on paper.
4. Press **Print**, and print from your browser's print window. Stickers print three across.

Sold and retired vehicles aren't listed on the sheet. To print one anyway, open its page and press **Print this sticker**.

To use a sticker, see [Scan a vehicle's QR sticker](scan-a-qr-sticker.md).

## Common problems

- **"No vehicles match these filters."** Press **Reset view**.
- A product isn't in the **Product** list: retired products aren't offered.
