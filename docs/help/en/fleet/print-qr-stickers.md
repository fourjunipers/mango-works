# Print QR stickers for your vehicles

Every vehicle has its own QR sticker. Scan it with a phone camera to open that vehicle. It only works for people signed in to your shop.

Who can do this: everyone in the shop.

## Print one sticker

1. Open the vehicle's page and scroll to **QR sticker**.
2. Press **Print this sticker**.

## Print a sheet of stickers

1. Open **Fleet** and press **Print stickers**.
2. To narrow the list, choose a **Product** and a **Sort by** order (**Most recently added** or **Plate**), then press **Apply**.
3. Tick the vehicles to print, or press **Select all** for everything listed. Only the ticked stickers appear on paper.
4. Press **Print**.

## Scanning

On the **Maintenance** tab press **Scan QR** and point the camera at the sticker: the vehicle opens as soon as the code is read. A phone's own camera app works too.

## Common problems

- **"No vehicles match these filters."** Press **Reset view**.
- **"That code isn't one of your vehicle stickers. Keep the camera on a sticker."** The camera read another QR code.
- **"The camera is blocked for this site. Allow it in the browser's site settings, then try again."**
- **"This browser can't use the camera here. Use your phone's camera app instead: it opens the same sticker."**
