# Mark a vehicle as sold

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps

1. Open the vehicle's page.
2. Under **Status**, choose **Sold** and save.
3. Confirm "Mark this vehicle as sold? It leaves the fleet list and can't be rented or booked. Its history is kept. You fill in the sale details next."
4. The page jumps to **Purchase and sale**: "Marked as sold. Fill in the sale date, price and buyer here, then save."
5. Fill in **Sale date**, **Sale price** and **Sold to**, and press **Save**.

## Purchase and sale details

**Purchase and sale** is at the bottom of the vehicle's page. Only owners and managers see it. It holds **Purchase date**, **Purchase price**, **Bought from**, **Sale date**, **Sale price** and **Sold to**. You can fill in the purchase details at any time.

If a vehicle has sale details but isn't marked Sold, the section says "This vehicle has sale details but its status isn't Sold. Set the status to Sold above to take it out of the fleet."

## Common problems

- **"This vehicle still has a block that ends in the future. End or remove it first."** A vehicle with future downtime or bookings can't be sold. End or remove its future blocks first.
- **"Enter an amount, such as 45000 or 45000.50."** Type the price as a number, with a dot for decimals.
- **"Enter a real date."** Check the date.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
