# Mark a vehicle as sold

Who can do this: owners and managers. Staff, mechanics and drivers can't choose **Sold**, and don't see the **Purchase and sale** section.

## Steps

1. Open the vehicle's page.
2. Under **Status**, choose **Sold** ("Sold. Can't be rented. After saving, fill in the sale details at the bottom of this page.") and press **Save**.
3. Confirm "Mark this vehicle as sold? It leaves the fleet list and can't be rented or booked. Its history is kept. You fill in the sale details next."
4. The page jumps to **Purchase and sale**: "Marked as sold. Fill in the sale date, price and buyer here, then save."
5. Fill in **Sale date**, **Sale price** (in your currency, for example **Sale price (THB)**) and **Sold to**, and press **Save**.

## Purchase and sale details

**Purchase and sale** is near the bottom of the vehicle's page: "Only owners and managers see this." It holds **Purchase date**, **Purchase price**, **Bought from**, **Sale date**, **Sale price** and **Sold to**. You can fill in the purchase details at any time. Saving these details never changes the vehicle's status.

If a vehicle has sale details but isn't marked Sold, the section says "This vehicle has sale details but its status isn't Sold. Set the status to Sold above to take it out of the fleet."

## Common problems

- **"This vehicle still has a block that ends in the future. End or remove it first."** A vehicle with future downtime or bookings can't be sold. End or remove its future downtime under **Downtime**. Bookings are changed from orders, which come in a later update.
- **"Enter an amount such as 200 or 1,234.50. Use a dot for decimals; a comma only between thousands (1,000). No spaces, and no more decimal places than the currency has."** Type the price with a dot for decimals (12.50, not 12,50) and no spaces.
- **"Enter a real date."** Check the date.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.

Sold by mistake? Choose **Active** under **Status** and press **Save**: see [Take a vehicle off the road](change-status.md).

For how to type numbers, see [Typing amounts, percentages and other numbers](../general/typing-numbers.md).
