# Add a closure (holiday or day off)

A closure marks days a location is shut, on top of its weekly opening hours.

## Who can do this

Owners and managers.

## Steps

1. Open **Locations** and choose the location.
2. Under **Closures**, fill in **First day** (required) and, for more than one day, **Last day**. Leave **Last day** blank for a single day.
3. Add a **Note** if you like, such as "Songkran".
4. Tick **Repeats every year** for a closure on the same dates each year.
5. Press **Add closure**.

The closure appears in the list (or "No closures." when there are none); yearly ones say "every year" and show no year. To take one off, press **Delete** next to it. It is removed straight away, with no question first.

## Common problems

- **"Enter a real date."** Check the date.
- **"The end must be after the start."** The last day is before the first day. (The same day for both is fine.)
- **"You can't make that change."** Only owners and managers can change locations. Ask one of them.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
