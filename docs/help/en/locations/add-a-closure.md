# Add a closure (holiday or day off)

A closure marks days a location is shut, on top of its weekly opening hours.

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps

1. Open **Locations** and choose the location.
2. Under **Closures**, fill in **First day** and, for more than one day, **Last day**.
3. Add a **Note** if you like, such as "Songkran".
4. Tick **Repeats every year** for a closure on the same dates each year.
5. Press **Add closure**.

The closure appears in the list; yearly ones say "every year". To take one off, press **Delete** next to it.

## Common problems

- **"Enter a real date."** Check the date.
- **"The end must be after the start."** The last day is before the first day.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
