# Add a location

Every shop starts with one location, marked **Main location**. Each location has its own time zone, address, opening hours and closures, and each vehicle belongs to one location.

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Steps

1. Open **Locations**. It is under **Shop admin**: in the sidebar on a computer, or on the **You and this device** page on a phone (see [Finding your way around](../account/switch-shop-and-device.md)).
2. Press **Add location**.
3. Fill in **Name** (required) and **Time zone**. The time zone decides opening hours, due dates and "today" at this location.
4. Add any of: **Address**, **Map link** (must start with https://), **Email**, **Phone**, **WhatsApp** and **LINE ID**. Phone numbers use the international format, such as +66850550247.
5. Press **Save**.
6. Set the location's hours and days off: see [Set opening hours](set-opening-hours.md) and [Add a closure](add-a-closure.md).

The **Moto Mangos market** field says "Set when markets arrive (Phase 3M)." Nothing to do there yet.

## Delete a location

1. Open the location.
2. Press **Delete location** and confirm "Delete this location? This can't be undone."

Move its vehicles to another location first (on each vehicle's **Details**).

## Common problems

- **"That name is already used in this shop."** Each location needs its own name.
- **"Use the international format, such as +66850550247."** Start the number with + and the country code, with no spaces.
- **"Enter a link that starts with https://"** Paste the full map link, for example from Google Maps' Share button.
- **"Enter an email address."** Check the email for typos.
- **"Choose a time zone from the list."** Pick a time zone from the list rather than typing one.
- **"Only an owner can change a location's time zone."** See [Change a location's time zone](change-time-zone.md).
- **"A shop keeps its main location."** The main location can't be deleted.
- **"It is still in use, so it can't be deleted."** Vehicles or other records still belong to this location. Move the vehicles to another location, then delete it.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
