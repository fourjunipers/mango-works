# Change a location's time zone

Who can do this: owners only. Managers see the time zone with the note "Only an owner can change the time zone."

## Steps

1. Open **Locations** and choose the location.
2. Choose the new **Time zone** from the list.
3. Press **Save**.
4. Confirm "Changing the time zone moves every opening hour and due date of this location. Save anyway?"

The time zone is per location, not per shop. **Shop settings** says "Time zone, address, opening hours and closures are set per location."

## Common problems

- **"Choose a time zone from the list."** Pick from the list rather than typing.
