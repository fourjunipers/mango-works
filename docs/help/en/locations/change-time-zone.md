# Change a location's time zone

## Who can do this

Owners only. Managers see the time zone as text, with the note "Only an owner can change the time zone." (A manager who adds a new location does choose its time zone then: see [Add a location](add-a-location.md).)

## Steps

1. Open **Locations** and choose the location.
2. Choose the new **Time zone** from the list.
3. Press **Save**.
4. Confirm "Changing the time zone moves every opening hour and due date of this location. Save anyway?" You are asked only when the time zone really changed.

The time zone is per location, not per shop. **Shop settings** says "Time zone, address, opening hours and closures are set per location.", with an **Open locations** link.

## Common problems

- **"Choose a time zone from the list."** Pick from the list rather than typing.
- **"Only an owner can change a location's time zone."** Your role isn't owner (or it changed while the page was open). Ask an owner.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
