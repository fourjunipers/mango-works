# Change your shop's name, country, currency and formats

**Shop settings** holds what every price, report and reminder uses: "Every price, report and reminder uses these. Nothing assumes a country."

## Who can do this

Owners and managers. Other roles don't see **Shop settings** in the menu.

## Steps

1. Open **Shop settings**. It is under **Shop admin**: in the sidebar on a computer, or on the **You and this device** page on a phone (see [Finding your way around](../account/switch-shop-and-device.md)). The **Shop** section opens first. The other sections (**Pricing and tax**, **Seasons**, **Price lists**, **Coupons**, **Extras**, **Booking rules**) have their own articles.
2. Change any of:
   - **Shop name**
   - **Country**: choose from the list.
   - **Currency**: choose from the list.
   - **Number and date format**: a language tag such as en-GB, th-TH or ja-JP. Start typing to see suggestions.
   - **Distances**: **Kilometres (km)** or **Miles (mi)**. Odometer readings are always stored in km and shown in this unit.
3. Press **Save**.

Time zone, address, opening hours and closures are set per location (the page links to them with **Open locations**): see [Add a location](../locations/add-a-location.md).

## The currency locks

Once money has been saved in the shop's currency, the currency can't change, and the field says "Locked: prices have been saved in this currency." The currency is fixed by the first of any of these: saving **Pricing and tax**, a product's prices or a price list; saving a vehicle's purchase and sale details; a tax or insurance record with a cost; a coupon with a fixed amount off or a minimum spend; or a booking made with a coupon.

## Common problems

- **"Required."** Fill in the field.
- **"Too long."** Shorten the text.
- **"Choose a country from the list."** Pick a country rather than typing one.
- **"Choose a currency from the list."** Pick a currency rather than typing one.
- **"Use a language tag such as en-GB."** Type a language code, a hyphen, then a country code.
- **"Choose km or miles."** Pick one of the two.
- **"The currency can't change once prices have been saved in it."** The currency is locked. Contact Mango Works if it really is wrong.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
