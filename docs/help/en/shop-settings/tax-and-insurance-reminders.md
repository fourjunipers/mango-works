# Set tax and insurance reminders

Reminders tell you when a vehicle's road tax or insurance is due for renewal. They show on the **Dashboard** under "Tax and insurance renewals due", and on the **Late** tab under "Overdue services and renewals" once the date has passed.

## Who can do this

Owners and managers change the setting. Everyone who has the **Dashboard** tab sees the reminders there.

## Steps

1. Open **Shop settings**.
2. Under **Tax and insurance reminders**, type the **Days before expiry**: up to 10 numbers separated by commas, for example 45, 30, 14, 7. Leave it blank for no reminders.
   The largest number also decides when a renewal first shows on the dashboard.
3. Choose a **Monthly digest**: **Off**, or **On day {day} of the month** (1 to 28), for one list of everything expiring that month. Its help says "Not sent yet: the setting is kept, and the digest comes in a later update."
4. Press **Save**. This section has its own **Save** button, separate from the shop details above it.

The expiry dates come from each vehicle's records: see [Record road tax, insurance or an inspection](../fleet/record-tax-and-insurance.md).

Today the reminders show on the dashboard only. Sending them by email or LINE, and sending the monthly digest, come in a later update.

## Common problems

- **"Enter up to 10 whole numbers from 1 to 365, separated by commas."** Check for decimals, numbers over 365, or more than 10 numbers. Each number is a whole number of days; the commas go between the numbers, never inside one.
- **"Choose a day from 1 to 28."** The digest day must exist in every month.
- **"You can't make that change."** Only owners and managers can change reminders.
- **"Couldn't save. Please try again."** Reload the page and try again.
