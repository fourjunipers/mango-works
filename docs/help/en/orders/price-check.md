# Check what is free and what it costs (New order price check)

The **New order** tab is a **Price check** for now. Pick a pickup and a return, and where the booking would come from. Every product shows how many are free and, when it can't be booked, why. Tap one to see its price, with your seasons, extras, a coupon and the returning-customer discount.

It only checks. Nothing is booked or held, and trying a coupon does not count a use. Making orders comes in Phase 3, on this same tab.

**Who can do this**

- Owners, managers and staff: the free counts and the prices.
- Mechanics and drivers: the free counts only, with no prices.

## Steps

1. Open the **New order** tab.
2. If your shop has more than one location, choose the **Location**.
3. Set the **Pickup** and the **Return**. They start as tomorrow at 10:00 to the day after, on the location's clock.
4. Under **Booked from**, choose one:
   - **The counter**: a booking staff make in the app. Your minimum notice does not apply.
   - **Your booking page**: a customer booking online. Your minimum notice applies.
   - **Moto Mangos**: a booking from Moto Mangos. Only vehicles listed on Moto Mangos can be booked there.
5. Press **Check**.
6. Each product shows "N of M free" (a service shows "A service: nothing to count"), and a reason in red if it can't be booked.
7. To see a price, press **Price** next to the product. The price opens below the list as **Check a price**, headed with the product and the channel. It starts with the dates you chose; you can change them there, and add extras, a **Coupon code** (press **Apply**) or tick **Returning customer**. See [Check a price](../products/check-a-price.md) for the breakdown.

A coupon is judged for the channel you chose. A coupon that is only for your booking page is refused under **The counter** and applied under **Your booking page**.

## Why a product can't be booked

- **"Too short: at least N days on this channel."** The rental is shorter than the minimum days: the walk-in minimum for **The counter**, the booked-ahead minimum online. See [Set your booking rules](../booking-rules/set-booking-rules.md).
- **"Too soon: the earliest pickup on this channel is …"** Your minimum notice (online only). Pick a later pickup, or choose **The counter**.
- **"Closed at the pickup time."** / **"Closed at the return time."** The location is closed then (opening hours or a closure).
- **"This location has no opening hours, so it takes no online bookings."** Set opening hours on the location. See [Set opening hours](../locations/set-opening-hours.md).
- **"Not listed on Moto Mangos."** The vehicle is not switched on for Moto Mangos.
- **"Moto Mangos has used all the units you list there for these dates."** Moto Mangos bookings already take every Instant and Request unit you list for this vehicle. Raise the counts on its Moto Mangos tab if you want to offer more.
- **"None free for these dates."** Every one is booked or blocked.
- **"None free. Overbooking is on, so staff could still save it as a shortage."** Staff can still take the booking at the counter once orders arrive, and a vehicle must be found before pickup. Online bookings are never overbooked.

## Common problems

- **"The return must be after the pickup."** Set a later return.
- **"Nothing to rent at this location yet."** The location has no vehicles, gear or services yet.
- **"Add a location first."**
- The price says **"Fill in the prices to check one."**: the product has no price yet.
- A coupon that isn't applied says why: see [Why a coupon wasn't applied](../coupons/coupon-not-applied.md).
