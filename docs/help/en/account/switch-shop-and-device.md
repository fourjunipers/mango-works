# Switch shop and set up this device

**You and this device** shows who you are signed in as, your role in this shop, and the choices saved on this device only.

## Switch to another shop

1. Open the menu and press **Switch shop**.
2. On **Choose a shop**, pick the shop.

**Switch shop** only shows if you belong to more than one shop.

## Set language, appearance and tab bar

1. Open **You and this device** from the menu.
2. Under **This device**, choose:
   - **Language**: English or Thai.
   - **Appearance**: **Match device**, **Light** or **Dark**.
   - **Tab bar on phones**: **Bottom** or **Top**. On a wide screen the tabs are in the sidebar instead.

These choices are saved on this device only. Set them again on another phone or computer.

## Install the app

On a phone, open the browser menu and choose "Add to Home screen". On a computer, use the install icon in the address bar. It's optional: everything also works in the browser.

## Sign out

Press **Sign out** in the menu.

## Which tabs you see

The staff app has four tabs: **Dashboard**, **Orders**, **New order** and **Maintenance**. Owners, managers and staff see all four. Mechanics see **Dashboard** and **Maintenance**, and open straight to **Maintenance**. Drivers see **Dashboard** and **Orders**. Only owners and managers see **Locations**, **Team** and **Shop settings**.
