# Switch shop and set up this device

## Finding your way around

**On a computer** (a wide screen), everything is in the sidebar on the left:

- At the top: the shop's name, with **Switch shop** under it when you belong to more than one shop.
- **Staff app**: the tabs your role uses (**Dashboard**, **Orders**, **New order**, **Maintenance**).
- **Shop admin**: **Products**, **Fleet** and, for owners and managers, **Locations**, **Team** and **Shop settings**.
- At the bottom: **You and this device**, your email, and **Sign out**.

**On a phone** (a narrow screen):

- The staff-app tabs sit in the tab bar, at the bottom or the top.
- The header at the top shows the shop's name and two icons: **Switch shop** (only when you belong to more than one shop) and **You and this device** (the person icon).
- The shop admin pages have no tab. Open **You and this device**: they are listed under its **Shop admin** section.
- **Sign out** is on the **You and this device** page.

## Switch to another shop

1. On a computer, press **Switch shop** under the shop's name in the sidebar. On a phone, press the **Switch shop** icon in the header.
2. On **Choose a shop**, pick the shop.

## Set language, appearance and tab bar

1. Open **You and this device**: at the bottom of the sidebar on a computer, or the person icon in the header on a phone.
2. Under **This device**, choose:
   - **Language**: English or Thai.
   - **Appearance**: **Match device**, **Light** or **Dark**.
   - **Tab bar on phones**: **Bottom** or **Top**. On a wide screen the tabs are in the sidebar instead.

These choices are saved on this device only. Set them again on another phone or computer.

**You and this device** also shows who you are signed in as and your role in this shop.

## Install the app

On a phone, open the browser menu and choose "Add to Home screen". On a computer, use the install icon in the address bar. It's optional: everything also works in the browser.

## Sign out

On a computer, press **Sign out** at the bottom of the sidebar. On a phone, open **You and this device** and press **Sign out**.

## Which tabs you see

Owners, managers and staff see all four staff-app tabs. Mechanics see **Dashboard** and **Maintenance**, and open straight to **Maintenance**. Drivers see **Dashboard** and **Orders**. Everyone sees **Products** and **Fleet**; only owners and managers see **Locations**, **Team** and **Shop settings**.
