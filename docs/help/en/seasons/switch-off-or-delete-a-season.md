# Change, switch off or delete a season

Who can do this: owners and managers.

## Change a season

1. Open **Shop settings** › **Seasons** and choose the season.
2. Change what you need.
3. Press **Save season**.

## Switch it off

1. Open the season.
2. Untick **Season on**: switched off, it stays saved and changes no price.
3. Press **Save season**. The list shows it as **Off**.

## Delete it

1. Open the season.
2. Press **Delete season** and confirm "Delete this season? This can't be undone."

The list says "Season deleted."

Changes to seasons are listed on the **History** tab of each product they cover.
