# Set a seasonal price

A season moves prices up or down by a percentage over a range of dates, on the part of a rental inside it. Each charged day belongs to the calendar day it starts on, so a 10-day rental with 3 days in the season gets the change on those 3 days only.

A season changes prices by rental length and flat fees only: never a fixed price, a free item or a deposit.

Who can do this: owners and managers. (Staff see the seasons that affect a product on its Pricing tab.)

## Steps

1. Open **Shop settings** and choose **Seasons**.
2. Press **New season**.
3. Type a **Name**, for example "Low season".
4. Choose the **First day** and **Last day**.
5. **Every year** starts ticked: the season repeats on the same days each year. Untick it for one year only. When ticked, the year is ignored, and the season may run over the new year (for example 20 December to 10 January).
6. Under **Price change**, choose the **Direction** (**Increase** or **Decrease**) and type the **Percentage**, for example 20 or 12.5. The change is worked out on the list price.
7. Under **Products**, choose **All products** or **Chosen products**. For chosen products, use **Search products** and tick them.
8. Leave **Season on** ticked.
9. Press **Create season**.

The **Seasons** list shows each season with its change (for example "20% more"), its dates and which products it covers.

Each product's Pricing tab lists the seasons that affect it, and **Check a price** shows a **Season** line with how many days fell inside. See [Check a price](../products/check-a-price.md).

## Common problems

- **"Required."** Fill in the name, days and percentage.
- **"Enter a real date."**
- **"The last day is before the first. Tick "Every year" for a season over the new year."**
- **"Enter a percentage such as 10 or 12.5. Use a dot for decimals, not a comma."**
- **"Enter a percentage above 0."**
- **"A decrease must be below 100%."**
- **"At most 1,000%."**
- **"Choose at least one product, or All products."**
- **"Check the season's details and try again."**

For how to type numbers, see [Typing amounts, percentages and other numbers](../general/typing-numbers.md).
